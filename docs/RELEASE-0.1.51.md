- X clips are back. The way the app found them had stopped working, so X never showed anything even when it was switched on. It now finds posts a different way and plays them as before. X is still off by default; turn it on in Settings.
- The first video arrives sooner. While nothing is on screen the app now looks in two places at once, waits less for a slow one, and gives up on a clip that is taking too long to start when another is ready behind it.
- Fewer clips in the wrong language. TikTok clips with no speech or caption are now judged by the creator's own profile, X clips by the language X records, and captions written with letters English never uses are caught too.
- New setting under Languages: "Only clips with a known language". Turn it on to skip any TikTok, Instagram, Facebook or X clip that gives no clue what language it is in. You will see fewer clips, and fewer surprises.
